# SpeedX POD (Proof of Delivery) Photo Standard

> SpeedX POD Standard / Guía de estándares POD / POD规则

This document has two parts:

- **Part A** transcribes the official SpeedX POD rules from `image_instructions.png`.
- **Part B** adds practical guidance for drivers. These are recommendations that build on the official rules. They are not part of the original SpeedX sheet.

---

## Part A: Official SpeedX POD Requirements (from source image)

Every POD photo is stamped automatically with:
- **TrkNo**: the tracking number, e.g. `SPXLAS005672605190013809`
- **Time**: the timestamp, e.g. `2026-05-24 17:35:01`
- **Location**: GPS coordinates, e.g. `36.137770, -115.089122`

### 1. Clear package label
*Estándares de foto de etiqueta / 清晰的面单照片*

- Take a clear, readable photo of the shipping label.
- **For irregular or small packages, the tracking number must at least be clearly visible.**
  *En paquetes irregulares o pequeños, al menos el número de guía debe verse claramente.*

### 2. Clearly capture the actual package placement
*Capturar claramente la ubicación real del paquete / 安全的包裹放置位置*

- Show where the package was actually left, such as on the doormat or beside the front door.
- Include enough of the surroundings (door, wall, steps) to make the location recognizable.

### 3. Capture the recipient address marker clearly and completely
*Capturar claramente el identificador completo de la dirección del destinatario / 门牌号以及公寓号*

- Photograph the house number, building number, or apartment/unit number. Examples: the number on a building facade, the unit number by the door, or the number on a mailbox post.
- **If no address marker is visible:** take a photo of the **building exterior** **plus** a **GPS/navigation screenshot** showing you are at the destination.
  *GPS Screenshot / 导航照片 / Captura de navegación móvil*

### 4. Special delivery scenarios

| Scenario | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Apartment Mailroom / Locker** *(Apartamento Sala de correo / Casillero / 公寓收发室/快递柜)* | **Mailroom:** the photo should show other companies' parcels in the same area, proving it is the shared package area. *(Muestra que los paquetes de otra empresa también están colocados en la misma área / 放置在包裹室时，需照到其他公司的包裹也放在同个区域)*<br>**Locker:** photograph the locker monitor/screen showing the **recipient name and address**. *(Muestra el nombre y la dirección del destinatario / 包裹柜的显示屏需要拍到收件人姓名与地址)* |
| **Deliver to customer or front desk** *(Entregar al cliente o en la recepción / 交付给客户或前台)* | **Only photograph the customer's hand holding the package. Do not photograph the customer's face.** *(Tome únicamente una foto de la mano del cliente; no tome fotos del rostro del cliente / 包裹交付给客人时，只能拍到手部，严禁拍到客人脸部)* |
| **Farm Area** *(Área agrícola / 农场区域)* | **The package must be placed inside a fenced area or another safe area.** *(El paquete debe colocarse dentro de un área cercada o un área segura / 投递到农场时，包裹需放置在围栏内/安全区域)* |

### 5. Prohibited deliveries ❌

| Prohibited | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Must not deliver to a mailbox!** *(¡No debe entregarse en el buzón de correo! / 严禁投递到邮箱！)* | **May result in fines from $1,000 to $5,000.** *(Puede resultar en multas de $1,000 a $5,000 / 投递到邮箱会产生$1000-$5000的罚款)* |
| **Must not place the package in a public or unsafe area!** *(¡No debe colocar el paquete en un área pública o insegura! / 严禁将包裹放置在公共/不安全区域！)* | Examples: community center entrances, open lobbies or hallways of public buildings, bare ground or gravel in the open. |
| **Must not deliver to USPS (Post Office)!** *(¡No debe entregarse en USPS (Oficina Postal)! / 严禁将包裹投递到邮局！)* | Never leave or hand SpeedX packages at a USPS facility. |

## Part B: Enhanced Driver Guidance (recommended additions)

A good POD should answer four questions without anyone having to ask the driver:
1. **Which package?** The label or tracking number is readable.
2. **Where?** The address marker matches the recipient address.
3. **Where exactly?** The package's final resting place is visible and safe.
4. **When?** The timestamp and GPS stamp are present and consistent with the route.

If one photo cannot answer all four, take more than one.

### B1. Recommended photo set per stop

| # | Shot | Purpose | Required? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Label close-up** with barcode and tracking number in focus | Identifies the package | Always |
| 2 | **Placement shot**, taken 1–2 m (3–6 ft) away, showing the package *and* the door or entry point in one frame | Proves placement and location in a single image | Always |
| 3 | **Address marker** (house, building, or unit number) | Proves the address is correct | Always, or the fallback below |
| 3-alt | **Building exterior + GPS/navigation screenshot** | Fallback when no number is visible | Only if #3 is impossible |
| 4 | **Scenario evidence** (locker screen, mailroom with other carriers' parcels, hand-off, fenced area) | Proves a special-case delivery was handled correctly | When applicable |

**Best practice:** where possible, take the placement shot so that the package, the door, *and* the house or unit number all appear in one frame. That single photo is the strongest evidence you can capture.

### B2. Photo quality checklist

- [ ] **In focus.** Tap to focus on the label or number, and hold still until the shutter fires.
- [ ] **Well lit.** At night or in dim light, turn on the flash or the phone flashlight. The label and number must still be readable. Avoid glare on glossy labels by tilting the phone slightly.
- [ ] **Not obstructed.** Keep fingers, the scanner, or vehicle parts out of the frame. Wipe the lens regularly.
- [ ] **Landscape or portrait that fits the subject.** Don't crop out the door or the number.
- [ ] **Package fully in frame.** Don't cut off part of the package.
- [ ] **No heavy zoom.** Move closer instead of using digital zoom, which blurs the detail.
- [ ] **Stamp intact.** The TrkNo, Time, and Location overlay must be visible and not covered. Always use the in-app camera, never the gallery or old photos.
- [ ] **Review before submitting.** Open the photo and confirm that the label and number can be read. Retake it if not.

### B3. Placement standards

- **Out of sight from the street when possible**: behind a planter, beside the door, inside a screen door, or under a porch roof.
- **Protected from weather**: under cover in rain or snow, never in puddles, sprinklers, or direct sun for sensitive items.
- **Not blocking** doors, stairs, walkways, or wheelchair ramps. Never put the package where it creates a trip hazard.
- **Label facing up and out** so the recipient can identify it.
- **Follow delivery notes first.** If the customer left instructions (back door, side gate, neighbor, leasing office), follow them and photograph the instructed spot.
- **Gated or fenced property**: deliver inside only if the access is legitimate (code provided or gate unlocked). Never throw packages over gates or fences.
- **Multi-package stops**: stack the packages together and make sure every label, or at least every tracking number, is visible. If they can't fit in one frame, take more photos.

### B4. Location and address verification

- **Compare the address marker to the label before leaving the package.** Check the house number, the street, *and* the unit or apartment number. A wrong unit in the right building is still a misdelivery.
- **Watch for look-alikes**: 1500 vs 1508, Unit 3A vs 3B, "Court" vs "Street", or a duplex with two numbers.
- **GPS accuracy**: take the POD at the delivery point, not from the vehicle or at the curb. The GPS stamp should match the address. If it doesn't (for example, a large rural lot), add the exterior + navigation screenshot.
- **Never take the POD before the package is placed**, and never reuse a photo from an earlier attempt or stop.

### B5. Privacy and conduct

- **Never photograph faces.** This covers customers, residents, children, and passers-by. For a hand-off, frame only the hands and the package.
- **Do not photograph inside homes** beyond the doorway, and avoid capturing other personal items, license plates, or open doors with interiors visible.
- **Do not photograph pets or people** through windows.
- **Respect posted signs** (no soliciting, private property, beware of dog) and any safety concerns. If a stop is unsafe, don't deliver: mark it as an exception and contact dispatch.

### B6. When delivery cannot be completed safely

Do **not** force a delivery that breaks the rules above. Use the app's exception or attempt flow instead, and still take a photo as evidence:

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| No safe spot (only public areas or exposed ground) | Mark as attempted / exception and photograph the location to show why |
| Address cannot be found or doesn't exist | Take a GPS screenshot plus photos of the surrounding numbers, then contact dispatch |
| Locked building, no mailroom or locker access | Photograph the locked entry and directory, then mark as an access issue |
| Package damaged on arrival | Photograph the damage *before* delivery and report it per company procedure |
| Customer refuses the package | Photograph the package at the door (no faces) and record the refusal |
| Signature or age-restricted item | Follow the hand-off rules only; never leave it unattended |

### B7. Common rejection reasons (avoid these)

- Blurry or dark photo; label or number unreadable
- Photo shows only the package with no surroundings (location can't be identified)
- Address or unit number missing, partial, or different from the label
- Package placed in a mailbox, at a USPS facility, or in a public or unsafe area
- Customer's face visible in a hand-off photo
- Photo taken from the vehicle or far from the GPS address
- Reused, duplicate, or gallery photos
- Multi-package stop with some labels not visible

## Quick Reference Card

**DO ✅**
- Readable label (at least the tracking number)
- Package + door/entry in one frame
- Clear house or unit number, or exterior + GPS screenshot
- Mailroom: show other carriers' parcels
- Locker: show the screen with name and address
- Hand-off: hands only
- Farm: inside a fence or safe area
- Use flash at night; review before submitting

**DON'T ❌**
- Mailbox delivery ($1,000–$5,000 fine)
- Public or unsafe areas
- USPS / Post Office
- Customer faces
- Blurry, dark, or reused photos
- Photos taken from the vehicle
